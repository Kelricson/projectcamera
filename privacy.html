Project Camera Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

About Project Camera

Project Camera is a personal iPhone photography workflow for organizing photographs of comics, cards, and collectibles in Google Drive.

Information Accessed

Project Camera accesses Google Drive using Google OAuth authorization to upload photographs to folders selected by the user.

How Information Is Used

Photographs are uploaded directly to the user's Google Drive account. Project Camera does not sell personal information or use photographs for advertising.

Data Storage and Sharing

Photographs are stored in the user's Google Drive account, subject to Google's privacy and security practices.

Project Camera does not operate a separate server that stores uploaded photographs.

Access and Revocation

The user can revoke Project Camera's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
